Boss Studio

Boss Studio is a personal desktop app made by and for The Wheezing Predator, a gaming streamer. It isn't sold or offered to the public: it runs on one PC and works only with the streamer's own recordings and own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Runs the on-stream overlay: timer, death counter, goal, rules and Twitch chat.
  • Transcribes finished stream recordings on the PC.
  • Helps write each episode's YouTube title, description and chapters, and draws thumbnails.
  • Uploads the finished episode to the streamer's own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services, with the title, description, chapters, thumbnail and playlist the streamer picked.

Your data

Boss Studio has no servers and no accounts of its own. Everything it keeps stays on the streamer's PC. Read the privacy policy for exactly what it uses and how to remove its access.
